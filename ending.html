To my buddy, ♡

I don't really know how to end this, to be honest.

I've spent so much time making all these little things for you, trying to find the right words, choosing the little details, and hoping that, somehow, all of this would make you smile. And now that I'm here, at the very last page, I realize that there are still so many things I could say.

But maybe not everything needs to be put into words.

I made this website because I wanted to give you something special for your birthday. Something that was yours, something you could look back on someday and smile at.

But there's one thing I want you to understand before you leave this little corner of the internet.

All the appreciation I've written on this website isn't just because it's your birthday. You don't become more deserving of kindness on just one particular day of the year. You're worthy of it on ordinary days, during your worst times, and on all those days when you might not feel particularly special at all.

Your birthday just gave me an excuse to put some of it into words.

I hope life gives you plenty of reasons to smile, people who make you feel safe being yourself, and moments that remind you how beautiful it is to be alive.

And when life doesn't feel so kind, I hope you remember that a difficult chapter doesn't make your whole story a sad one.

You deserve so much good, Kawi. More than I could ever fit into a website.

And if, years from now, you ever open this website again, I hope you smile at the memories. I hope you laugh at some of the things I've written. Maybe you'll even cringe a little. 🤧

But most of all, I hope you'll remember that there's someone who's really, really grateful to have you in his life.

Thank you for being a part of my life, Buddy. Thank you for being you.

I can't promise that I'll always have the perfect words to say, or that I'll always know how to make things better. But I'll do my best to treat you with the love, honesty, and respect that our friendship deserves.

And I hope we get to make plenty more memories to look back on someday.

Until then, keep being your wonderfully imperfect self. Keep growing, keep laughing, keep discovering things you love, and don't forget to make room for your own happiness along the way.

I'm really glad it's you, Buddy. ♡

Happy birthday, once again. 🤍
